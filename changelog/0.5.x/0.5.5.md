# 0.5.5 — 2026-10-25

## Changed

- **Every tool's `content[]` escapes RIS-authored text** ([#79](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/79)): titles, summaries, keywords, and notes no longer render as emphasis, code, lists, or raw HTML, and URLs are links. The legislation, gazette, drafts, and announcements headings hold only the document number (`ris_track_changes`: the lead case number, else the document number); the title moves to its own `**Title:**` line. `structuredContent` is unchanged.
- **`ris_lookup_citation` searches again without a cited year RIS's title lacks** ("VwGVG 2014 §28", "BFA-VG 2014", "GSpG 1989"), taking only the law filed under the bare abbreviation and first promulgated no later than the cited year; `resolution_note` says the year was dropped ([#80](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/80)).
- **`ris_lookup_citation` reads the short form after a long title** ("StaatsanwaltschaftsG - StAG §35" → StAG § 35) ([#81](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/81)).
- **`ris_lookup_citation` sets aside the version a law is cited in** ("ABGB idF 2. ErwSchG §254", "ABGB aF §140"): the version searched stays the one in force on the requested date, `resolution_note` says the cited version was not applied and that `in_force_as_of` reads another date, and a gazette number inside the clause no longer makes the cite a gazette citation ([#82](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/82), [#86](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/86)).
- **`ris_lookup_citation` answers EU acts cited by number ("RL 2006/123/EG", "VO (EG) 178/2002") or as `EG-Vertrag` with the EUR-Lex pointer**, without a RIS request ([#83](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/83)).
- **`ris_lookup_citation` searches share one 48 s budget** measured from the first request, so a lookup that chains searches settles inside the client's 60 s; an exhausted budget ends in `upstream_timeout` ([#85](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/85)).
