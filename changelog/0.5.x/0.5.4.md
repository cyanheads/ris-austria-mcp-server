# 0.5.4 — 2026-10-24

## Changed

- **`RIS_CONTENT_BASE_URL` defaults to `https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at`**: constructed `content_urls` and `authentic_pdf_url` use it, and `ris_get_document` and `ris://document/{application}/{documentNumber}` fetch from it, a `document_url` on `www.` included, since a passed URL is read from the configured host as before ([#75](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/75)).
- **`RIS_*` config is parsed at startup**, so a bad value stops the server behind the config banner instead of failing each call ([#75](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/75)).
- **`ris_lookup_citation` reads more `norms_cited` shapes** ([#73](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/73), [#76](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/76), [#77](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/77)):
  - a trailing `;` is dropped ("AbgEO §53;"), as is a `.` or `,` right after a section number or sub-provision ("DSG §1.", "B-VG Art7,");
  - a parenthesized Absatz after the number or the Artikel keyword is a sub-provision ("Art 6(1) B-VG", "DSG §6(1)"), as is any sub-provision attached to an abbreviation-first number ("IPRG §4Abs.1", "VfGG §7Abs2");
  - `Z 2` or `Z. 2` after the number is a Ziffer in either order ("DSG §4 Z 2", "§ 6 Z. 2 DSG"), and after an abbreviation-first number a spaced capital is an OGH index letter, dropped ("ABGB §1330 A" → § 1330); a spaced lowercase letter, or section-first any spaced letter but a Ziffer, still joins the number ("ZPO §448 b" → § 448b, "Art 15 A B-VG" → Art 15A);
  - an ordinal-led abbreviation resolves ("1. COVID-19-JuBG");
  - a bare short title with a hyphen suspended before "und" is a norm ("Steuer- und Zollkoordination 2004");
  - an OGH entry joining full norms with ". " resolves its first norm and names the rest in `resolution_note` ("ZPO §179. ZPO §275 Abs2").
- **`ris_lookup_citation` ranks the cited abbreviation without its year** when the law's short title carries that year, so "AlVG 1977" resolves to AlVG, not the first title hit ([#78](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/78)).

## Fixed

- **`ris_get_document` and the document resource fail a bot-check page as `content_host_blocked`**: non-retryable `ConfigurationError` whose recovery names `RIS_CONTENT_BASE_URL`; a 503 without that page stays a retryable `upstream_error` ([#75](https://github.com/cyanheads/ris-austria-mcp-server/issues/75)).
- **`ris_lookup_citation` answers EC Treaty cites (`EG`, `EGV`) and pre-2008 `E`/`M` treaty CELEX numbers as EU misses** with an EUR-Lex link; a title search for "EG" matched EC agreements with third countries.
